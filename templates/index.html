Forecasting Limitations

  • Historical dataset covers 2014–2017.
  • External factors such as holidays, promotions and market changes are not included.
  • Moving Average may respond slowly to sudden sales changes.
  • Linear Regression assumes a linear relationship between time and sales.
  • Actual sales may differ significantly from predicted sales.
  • The forecast range is based on historical errors and does not guarantee future results.
